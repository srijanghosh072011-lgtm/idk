Before You Call

Quick answers.

What should I do while I wait for a plumber?

For leaks or bursts: turn off the main water supply (usually in the basement, near the front of the house). For gas smell: leave the building and call us from outside. For no heat: open cupboard doors under sinks to keep pipes from freezing.

Will I get the same technician every time?

Wherever possible, yes — we keep service records on your home, and if your preferred tech is available, we'll send them. Every one of our technicians is licensed and follows the same standards.

How do I pay? Do you offer financing?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit, cheque and e-transfer. For larger installs, we offer flexible monthly financing through our lending partners — ask for details when booking.
