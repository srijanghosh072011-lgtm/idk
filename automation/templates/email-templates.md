# Email Templates — Ghosh Designs

Reference copies of every email the automation sends. The actual sending logic lives in `Code.gs`. Use these when you need to write the first cold email by hand (the only one the system doesn't auto-send).

Placeholders:
- `{FirstName}` — contact's first name
- `{Company}` — business name
- `{CalendlyLink}` — your booking link

---

## First Cold Email (you send manually)

**Subject:** Quick question about {Company}

```
Hey {FirstName},

I was looking up [niche] businesses in Regina and came across {Company}. I noticed your website [doesn't exist / could use some updating] — I help businesses like yours get a site that actually brings in leads.

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call this week?

Best,
Srijan Ghosh
Ghosh Designs
```

After you send this, set their status to **Contacted** and fill in **Date Contacted**. The automation handles the rest.
