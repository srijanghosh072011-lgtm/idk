Who we are

Most beaches don't die of one disaster. They die of a thousand small habits nobody noticed.

Tideline is a coastal conservation collective for the moment before it's too late. We organise cleanups, log every kilogram we pull off the sand, and turn that data into the local policy that stops the litter at its source.

We've seen what happens when a coastline's reality lags behind its postcard. Tourists notice. Reefs starve. Towns empty out. We close that gap — one tide, one habit, one signature at a time.

The fix

Everything the tide needs to turn — in four moves.

01
Show up to a cleanup
Two hours, one morning, measurable results. A 30-person crew clears roughly a tonne from a single stretch of sand — and the data we log drives policy.
02
Refuse single-use
The cheapest cleanup is the bottle that never exists. One reusable bottle replaces an average of 156 plastic ones a year.
03
Back the policy
Bag bans, deposit schemes and producer-responsibility laws cut coastal litter by up to 40% where they pass. Your signature scales further than your hands.
04
Teach one person
Every volunteer we survey traces it back to a single conversation. Bring a friend to one cleanup and you've roughly doubled your lifetime impact.
