[ 08 — FAQ ]

Honest answers, no fine print.

Still have a question? Email me at hello@pipecraft.studio and I'll reply same-day.

Do you only build for plumbers? +

Plumbing is the core niche, but I happily build for adjacent home services — HVAC, drain, water heater, septic, restoration. The playbook transfers cleanly.

What's actually included in the flat price? +

Strategy call, custom design in Figma (every page), hand-coded build, on-page SEO, contact + lead form, click-to-call wiring, analytics, deployment, and 30 days of free post-launch tweaks.

Will I be able to update the site myself? +

Yes. By default I ship with a lightweight CMS (or WordPress, if you prefer) so you can edit copy, swap photos, and add service pages without touching code.

What if I already have a site? +

A redesign + rebuild is the most common project. I migrate your content, preserve your URLs and rankings, and modernize the design and performance underneath.

How do I get started? +

Send a quick message about your business and I'll reply within a day with a calendar link, a rough quote, and the next available start date.
