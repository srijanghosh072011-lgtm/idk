FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Do you offer emergency service after hours?

Yes — we're on call 24/7, 365 days a year. Burst pipes, no heat, gas smells — we dispatch a licensed tech any time of day, any day of the year. Call Us and we'll confirm an ETA immediately.

Are your prices upfront? Any hidden fees?

We quote flat-rate pricing before any work starts. You see the number, you approve it, and that's what you pay — even if the job takes longer than expected.

Do you work with SaskEnergy rebates & financing?

Yes. We help our customers claim available rebates on qualifying high-efficiency furnaces and heat pumps, and we offer monthly financing for installs through trusted partners.

How long does a typical furnace install take?

Most residential furnace replacements are completed in a single day — typically 4–8 hours depending on your home. We'll confirm the timeline during the free on-site quote.

Do you service commercial properties?

Absolutely. We handle commercial plumbing, heating, and cooling for offices, restaurants, retail, and new builds. Ask about our maintenance contracts for predictable pricing.

Is my quote free?

Yes — quotes on new installations (furnaces, ACs, water heaters, etc.) are always free and no-obligation. Diagnostic service calls have a small flat fee that's waived if you proceed with the recommended repair.
