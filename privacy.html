Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Ghosh Designs ("we," "us," or "our") operates https://ghoshdesigns.ca (the "Site"). This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. We comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

When you fill out the contact form on this Site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message you send us (anything you choose to write about your business)

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. We do not run ad networks. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Why we collect it

We use this information for one purpose only: to reply to your enquiry and, if we end up working together, to communicate with you about your project. That's it.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, a form-handling service, which passes them on to our business email inbox. Formspree stores a copy of each submission on its servers under its own privacy policy. From our side, the message lives in our email inbox.

The Site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information (IP address, browser type, timestamp) for security and performance purposes. We do not access or use these logs for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as the conversation is active, plus a reasonable period afterward (typically up to 2 years) in case you reach back out. After that, we delete them.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what we have on you. Email us and we'll tell you exactly what information we hold.
  • Correct it. If anything's wrong, we'll fix it.
  • Delete it. Ask us to delete your information and we will, within 30 days, unless we're legally required to keep it.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@ghoshdesigns.ca.

Third-party services

This Site loads content from a small number of outside services. Because your browser fetches that content directly, each of them may receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web traffic:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces.
  • Unsplash — photographs.
  • Tailwind CSS — page styling.
  • Formspree — contact form handling. This is the only one that receives what you type into the form.

Apart from Formspree, none of these services receive any information you submit through our contact form.

Children's privacy

This Site is intended for business owners and is not directed at anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be noted on the homepage for at least 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@ghoshdesigns.ca.
